About the project

Rome in Two Takes explores how cinema has represented Rome. Through six films and their locations, the project traces two complementary itineraries: Historical Journey and Women & Urban Space.

We developed the website for the Information Modeling and Web Technologies course within the Master's Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna.

The team

We developed the concept, selected the films and planned both itineraries together. We also designed the website's structure and its shared visual identity. Each of us then took responsibility for specific parts of the project.

Visual styles

The timeline at the top of each page lets you explore seven visual themes. Each uses typography, colour and cinematic references from a different period to give the same itineraries a distinct look.
