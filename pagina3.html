03

Image Sources

The images and film stills selected to illustrate the cinematic scenes are taken from various online sources, including film blogs and screenshots from streaming platforms. This material is used exclusively for educational, study and visual analysis purposes within a secure university environment. No visual content is used for commercial purposes or for personal gain.

04

Use of AI

Some descriptive text and parts of the programming code were generated with the help of Artificial Intelligence (LLM) tools. AI was also used as a technical aid to merge, combine and harmonise the various blocks of code (particularly in CSS and JavaScript) that we had written separately. AI was used to provide technical support and linguistic proofreading, but it did not replace the original concept, the creative choices, the tour design or the attention to the user experience, all of which remain entirely our own work.

05

Travel and Locations

The virtual tour itineraries have not been physically tested on site by the authors of the university project. Although their accuracy has been verified using tools such as Google Maps, we accept no responsibility for the actual accessibility of the locations. We strongly recommend that you check travel arrangements, routes and the actual existence of the sites via official external sources before any visit.

06

The Tours

The two proposed itineraries, Historical and Feminist, offer a cinematic and evocative exploration of the city of Rome through scenes from films. Despite the thorough preliminary study and research, the app is in no way intended to replace a professional or certified tour guide. The itineraries and narratives are an artistic and cultural reinterpretation of these cinematic locations.

07

Style Choices

The interactive section that allows you to change the site’s appearance using different historical styles is both a technical and a design exercise. For this reason, some stylistic and visual choices are deliberately exaggerated or taken to extremes to test the potential of HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There may be representations based on visual stereotypes, used solely for the purposes of aesthetic exploration and visual impact.
